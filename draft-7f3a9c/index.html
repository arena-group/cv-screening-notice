About this notice This notice explains how your personal information is used when you apply for a role with a company in the Arena Group, including how an AI tool is used to help screen applications. The company advertising the role (the "hiring entity") is named in the job advert and listed in the Annex, with its contact details. The hiring entity and AMEA Gulf Limited, Dubai Branch ("AMEA") are jointly responsible for your information. AMEA sets up and runs the screening tool and decides how it is configured; the hiring entity decides who to interview and hire. You can contact either of them to exercise your rights, and they will work together to respond. 2. The information we use • Your name, contact details and location. • Your CV and anything else you submit: work history, education, qualifications, skills and languages. • Information passed to us by LinkedIn when you apply through its platform: [confirm fields]. • The AI tool's assessment of how well your application matches the role, and recruiters' notes and decisions. • If you progress: interview notes, assessment results and references. • Before an offer: your right to work, nationality and immigration documents, where the law requires us to check them. We do not ask for information about your health, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation or other sensitive matters, and we ask you not to include it in your CV. The AI tool is configured not to use or infer this kind of information [confirm]. 3. How the AI screening works When you apply, the text of your CV is extracted and compared against the requirements the hiring manager sets for the role, such as skills, experience, qualifications and languages. The tool then sorts applications into those that appear to match the role, those that need a closer look, and those that appear not to match, for a recruiter to review. This affects whether your application moves to the next stage. The tool does not use your name, photo, age or contact details in its assessment [confirm]. The tool runs on Microsoft's Azure OpenAI service within the Arena Group's own secure Microsoft environment. Your information is not used to train or improve the AI model. The tool is classed as a high-risk AI system under the EU AI Act because it is used to filter and evaluate job applications. We are telling you that your application is subject to its use. 4. Human review and your rights about AI decisions We aim for a recruiter to review every outcome the tool produces. Where the volume of applications means an application is not taken forward without a person reviewing it, that is a decision made solely by automated means. If this happens to you, we will tell you, and you can: • give us your views on the decision; • ask for a person to review it; and • challenge it. You can also ask for a clear explanation of the role the AI tool played and the main reasons for the decision. To do any of these, email hr.me@arenagroup.com. We will reply within one month. 5. Who we share your information with • The hiring entity, and other group companies involved in the vacancy. • Microsoft, which provides the systems we use (SharePoint, Dataverse, Teams and Azure OpenAI) and acts only on our instructions. • LinkedIn, if you apply through it. LinkedIn is responsible for the information you give it under its own privacy policy. • Professional advisers, regulators, courts and public authorities where the law requires it. We do not sell your information. 7. International transfers Your application is stored on Microsoft systems in the European Union. If the role is with a group company in the UAE or Saudi Arabia, your information is sent there. Neither country is recognised by the UK or EU as providing adequate protection, so we use the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses to protect it. You can ask us for a copy of these safeguards. If you are applying from the UAE or Saudi Arabia, see the Annex. 8. Your rights You have the right to: • access your information and get a copy; • have inaccurate information corrected; • have your information deleted, or its use restricted, in some circumstances; • object to our use of your information based on legitimate interests, including the AI screening; • receive information you gave us in a reusable format, in some circumstances; and • the rights about automated decisions in section 4. To use any of these rights, contact the hiring entity at the address in the Annex, or AMEA at hr.me@arenagroup.com. We will respond within one month. 10. Complaints If you are unhappy with how we have used your information, please complain to us first at hr.me@arenagroup.com. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and tell you the outcome. You can also complain to the data protection regulator where you live or work, or where the hiring entity is based. In the UK this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Other regulators are listed in the Annex. 11. Changes to this notice We will update this notice if the screening process changes. The version date is shown at the top.
